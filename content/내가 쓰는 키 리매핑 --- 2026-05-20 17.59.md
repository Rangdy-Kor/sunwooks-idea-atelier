# 내가 쓰는 키 리매핑 --- 2026-05-20 17.59

---

- Caps Lock ==> Language Switch
- Left Ctrl ==> Left Win
- Left Win ==> Left Alt
- Left Alt ==> Left Ctrl
- Right Alt ==> Hanja
- Right Ctrl ==> Left Win + Left Shift + F23
- Right Shift ==> Backspace
- Backspace ==> Delete
- Delete ==> Caps Lock
